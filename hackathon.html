The idea

Start with a problem you care about.

Then give it a real-world shape.

The NACOS South East Hackathon is a chance to move from “what if?” to “here’s what I built.” Bring a project idea, explain the problem behind it and put your skills to work. Your idea can be at an early stage; the application asks you to describe what you want to create.

Meet us at Ogbonnaya Onu Polytechnic, Aba, during the NACOS South East Convention, 20th–24th October, 2026.
